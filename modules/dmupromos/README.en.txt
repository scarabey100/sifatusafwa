1 - Installation 

The module requires no special handling: 

- Copy the files in the directory "modules" of PrestaShop. 
- Install the module from your administration area
- It is immediately available in the "Catalog" under the name "Discount Campaigns" 

2 - Use 

The Home of the module is a list of promotional campaigns configured.
You can add a campaign, modify or delete.
Each campaign have a start date and end date.

By clicking on a campaign or on the magnifying glass icon, you arrive on the interface for awarding promotions.
In the left column, you are looking for products that you want to assign promotions.
Enter a discount percentage or amount and select the products concerned.
By clicking the "Apply Promotion", the products will be automatically added to the right column with the correspondingly reduced.

You can remove the promotion in the right column by selecting the desired products and clicking "Delete these reductions"
